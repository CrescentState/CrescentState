👨‍💻 About Me
Hi! I’m Alen John, a final-year B.Tech Artificial Intelligence & Data Science student at Muthoot Institute of Technology and Science (MITS), Kerala.

I don’t build tutorials. I build real, working systems — and I own every decision in them: why the stack was chosen, how it fails, and what happens next.

“Code is only half the work. The other half is knowing why it exists, and making it survive contact with reality.”

🧠 How I Think
Systems over scripts — I architect pipelines and agents that hold up under failure, not demos that fall over on a second run.
AI where it belongs — I use LLMs where they shine (narration, synthesis, understanding) and deterministic logic where it matters (decisions, money, scoring). I care about where I chose not to use AI as much as where I did.
Ship it, don’t show it — a project isn’t done until it’s deployed, tested, documented, and live for anyone to try.
🔭 Currently Focused On
Multi-agent orchestration and reliable AI pipelines (LangGraph, FastAPI)
Building resilient systems: caching, rate limiting, failover, graceful degradation
Deeper ML: RAG, evaluation metrics, risk modeling
Real users and real feedback — turning projects into products
🎯 Goal
To become an AI engineer who can be handed a problem with no spec — and return a system that runs in production.

☕ Fun Fact
I built my risk engine’s rate limiter because an API kept throttling me — and that failure became its best feature.
